LALAANGAN

Every Step Finds a Story.

A world of stories awaits beyond your doorstep.

Inspired by Sabah's folklore, culture, and hidden wonders, Lalaangan invites you to step outside, explore real-world locations, collect legendary creatures, and embark on quests.

Every path leads to discovery. Every creature holds a story. Every step brings you closer to the wonders of Sabah.

Support

Creatures aren't appearing

Check that Location access is allowed and that you're outdoors. Creatures appear when you're near a discovery spot.

My steps aren't counting

Keep the game open while walking. Step counting in the web version needs the screen on.

Is it safe to play while walking?

Always look up from your phone near roads, the beach and the river. The game will buzz when something is nearby.

Location

Checking…

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the icon next to the web address.
  2. Open Permissions, then Location.
  3. Choose Allow, then reload Lalaangan.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Location Services.
  2. Turn on Location Services and set Safari Websites to While Using.
  3. Return to Lalaangan and reload.
